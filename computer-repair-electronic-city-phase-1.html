You carry the laptop in. The person at the counter looks at it and says what failed.

What that bench takes

A cracked, dim, flickering, or black panel is checked, and the screen is changed when the panel is the fault. A pack that swells, dies early, or will not hold a charge is changed. Keys that stick, repeat, or stay dead, and a spill across the key mat, are a keyboard job. A drive that has turned slow or is failing is changed for an SSD or another drive. Heat in the palm rest, a fan that will not quiet down, or a shutdown with no warning is followed to the fan and the heat sink. Pop-ups and files you cannot open are handled by taking malware off and cleaning the system.

Why the order helps

You hear the fault before money changes hands. A dim screen is not written up as a new battery, and a hot palm rest is not written up as a new drive. The quote names the job that matches the machine you brought. That is the benefit of stopping at the counter first.

No figure is printed here. Call or WhatsApp 099724 47766, say what the laptop is doing, and ask for the quote before any paid work. You choose after you hear which part, if any, will be fitted.
